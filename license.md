## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is this legal?</b></summary>

Scraping public data is generally legal, but it may violate Facebook's Terms of Service. This tool is intended for **educational and research purposes only**. Do not use it to spam users or collect private data.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need a Facebook account?</b></summary>

Yes, it is highly recommended to use a "burner" Facebook account. Scraping without an account will quickly trigger a login wall, and using your main account risks getting it banned.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I scrape private groups?</b></summary>

No. This scraper only works on **public pages** that are visible to everyone. It cannot bypass privacy settings or access private groups.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I avoid getting blocked?</b></summary>

You must use proxies. The scraper supports proxy lists. We recommend using high-quality residential proxies to mimic real user traffic.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug?</b></summary>

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include the URL of the page you tried to scrape and the error traceback.
</details>
